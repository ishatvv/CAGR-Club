Questions people ask first

What background do I need?
School-level arithmetic and curiosity about businesses. The first roadmap starts with how to read a profit and loss statement.
Which roles does this prepare for?
Sell-side research associate, buy-side analyst and credit analyst roles. Much of it also carries over to investment banking and corporate finance interviews.
How is this different from a textbook?
Every idea comes with a question to answer and a tool to try. You learn a ratio by using it on numbers, then by spotting a forecast that breaks it.
How much time does it take each day?
The daily set takes about ten minutes. Role tracks assume eight to ten hours a week.
Is any of this investment advice?
No. Companies in examples are invented, and nothing here recommends buying or selling a security.
